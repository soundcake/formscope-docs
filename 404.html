FormScope documentation

Report on the answers your forms already collect, without creating a Jira custom field for every question.

FormScope turns answers from native forms in Jira Service Management Cloud into searchable tables, charts, completeness reports and Jira dashboard gadgets. It reads answers that are not linked to Jira fields, combines them with request details, and compares equivalent questions across forms and service projects.

What you can do

  • Search form answers alongside request key, summary, status, assignee, project, request type and dates.
  • Build tables and charts from questions that are not linked to Jira custom fields.
  • Map equivalent questions from different forms, such as "Department", "Business unit" and "Cost centre", into one reporting field.
  • Count requests or form submissions, total and average number answers, and group by month or other date periods.
  • Find submitted forms that are missing relevant information, without counting questions hidden by form conditions.
  • Save reports, share them with other FormScope users, and add them to Jira dashboards.
  • Export filtered results to CSV.
Every report only shows requests the viewer can open in Jira, and forms with view restrictions only to people allowed to view them. See Permissions.
