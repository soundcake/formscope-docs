Install and set up

FormScope works with the native forms you already use in Jira Service Management Cloud. Keep your forms exactly as they are.

Install

  1. A Jira admin installs FormScope from the Atlassian Marketplace. Every installation starts with a free evaluation, then continues as a subscription billed by Atlassian.
  2. Open Jira settings > Apps and select Configure for FormScope, or open Apps > FormScope from the Jira sidebar.

Choose forms and questions

You decide exactly what FormScope reports on. On the Get started tab:

  1. Service project. Search for the service project that uses the form.
  2. Form. Choose one of the project's forms. A data source covers one form, including every version of it over time.
  3. Questions. Pick the questions you want to report on. FormScope stores just these answers. For number questions, set a unit such as USD or items.
  4. Which requests. Index requests created on or after a date (default: 12 months ago) or from any date.
  5. Access checks. Keep Check each viewer's access to every form on (recommended). See Permissions.
  6. Review and start. FormScope summarises what it will read in one sentence. Select Start indexing.

Indexing runs in the background while your team keeps working, and the Indexing tab shows live progress. Reports start working as soon as the first batch is ready and fill in automatically as indexing completes. In our testing, a few hundred requests indexed in under a minute.

Create your first report

Open Apps > FormScope > Create report, pick a template, choose your questions and save. See Reports and mappings.

Who can use FormScope

Everyone with Jira access can open FormScope reports by default. To choose specific groups, go to Settings. Jira admins always have access, and every report follows each person's Jira permissions: see Permissions.
