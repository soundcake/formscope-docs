Supported forms

  • Product: Jira Service Management Cloud. Not Data Center.
  • Forms: native forms (formerly ProForma) attached to requests in service projects, read through the official Forms REST API. Forms created on request types or added to requests by agents are both supported.
  • Projects: company-managed and team-managed service projects.
  • Question types: short and long text, paragraph, email and URL text, number, date, date and time, time, single choice (radio and dropdown), multiple choice (checkboxes and multi-select), single and multiple users, and attachments (counted, file names are not stored).
  • Conditional sections: show and hide conditions, including the newer field checks. When a condition can't be evaluated reliably, FormScope reports "can't be assessed" instead of guessing.
  • Not supported: Assets object questions (shown as answered or not answered only), choices from external data connections (shown with their identifier when no label is available), forms outside Jira Service Management, and native JQL search on form answers.
  • Restricted forms: see Permissions.
