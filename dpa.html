Data processing agreement

FormScope - Forms Reports & Analytics for Jira. Last updated 19 November 2026.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement under which you ("Customer") use FormScope, provided by LimeVibe Limited, registered in England and Wales, company number 06872411 ("LimeVibe"). It applies automatically to Personal Data that LimeVibe processes on the Customer's behalf through FormScope, with no separate signature needed. Terms such as controller, processor, personal data, processing and data subject have the meanings given in the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR (together, "Data Protection Law").

1. Roles and scope

The Customer is the controller and LimeVibe is the processor of the Personal Data described in Schedule 1. LimeVibe processes it only to provide FormScope and only on the Customer's documented instructions. The agreement, this DPA and the Customer's configuration of FormScope (the projects, forms, questions, reports and settings its admins choose) are those instructions. LimeVibe will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

2. Confidentiality

LimeVibe ensures that anyone it authorizes to process the Personal Data is bound by confidentiality.

3. Security

LimeVibe implements the technical and organisational measures in Schedule 2, appropriate to the risk of the processing.

4. Sub-processors

The Customer authorizes Atlassian as LimeVibe's sub-processor, as the provider of the Forge platform that hosts, runs and stores FormScope. LimeVibe will give notice on this page before adding or replacing a sub-processor, giving the Customer the opportunity to object, and will bind each sub-processor to data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA. LimeVibe remains responsible for its sub-processors.

5. International transfers

Personal Data stays in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage, which keeps app data in the same location as the Customer's Atlassian site. Any transfer of Personal Data outside the UK or the EEA takes place only under a transfer mechanism recognised by Data Protection Law, such as the safeguards in Atlassian's own data processing terms.

6. Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing, LimeVibe assists the Customer in responding to data subject requests and in meeting its obligations on security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation. Jira admins can view, export and delete the data FormScope stores directly in the app at any time.

7. Personal data breaches

LimeVibe notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's Personal Data, with the information the Customer reasonably needs to meet its own obligations.

8. Deletion and return

The Customer can delete all data FormScope stores at any time from Settings > Data management. When FormScope is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data after 28 days. LimeVibe keeps no copies of Customer Personal Data outside FormScope's Forge storage.

9. Information and audits

LimeVibe makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allows for and contributes to audits by the Customer or an auditor it mandates, on reasonable notice and subject to confidentiality.

10. Order of precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the agreement on the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

Schedule 1: Details of processing

Subject matter and purposeProviding FormScope's reporting on native Jira Service Management form answers to the Customer.
DurationFor as long as FormScope is installed, plus the platform deletion period after uninstalling.
Nature of processingReading, indexing, storing, aggregating, displaying and exporting data, as configured by the Customer.
Types of personal dataAnswers to the form questions the Customer selects (which may contain personal data if its forms collect it); request details including summary, assignee name and Atlassian account ID; account IDs and display names of people named in user questions; account IDs of report owners and admins who configure FormScope.
Data subjectsThe Customer's Jira users, its service desk customers and any people named in its form answers.
Sub-processorsAtlassian (Forge platform): hosting, compute and storage.

Schedule 2: Security measures

  • Runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, with no external servers and no outbound connections.
  • Each installation's data is held in its own Forge storage, encrypted at rest by Atlassian.
  • Every request checks the licence, app access, report access and the viewer's Jira permissions on the server, using Atlassian's signed context.
  • Least-privilege scopes: read access to Jira work items and users, app storage and personal data reporting.
  • Logs contain identifiers, counts and error codes only, never form answers or names.
  • Weekly personal data reporting to Atlassian, erasing closed accounts.
  • Dependencies are scanned for known vulnerabilities before each release.
