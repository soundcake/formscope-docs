Privacy policy

FormScope - Forms Reports & Analytics for Jira. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who we are

LimeVibe Limited ("we"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 06872411), provides FormScope, an app for Jira Service Management Cloud. Contact: contact@limevibelimited.com.

How FormScope works

FormScope runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It reads data from your Jira site using Atlassian's APIs and stores what it needs in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage that belongs to your installation. FormScope does not send data to any server operated by us or by anyone else. We do not have access to the answers FormScope stores for your site.

Data FormScope processes for your site

  • Selected form answers. Only answers to the questions a Jira admin chooses. These can include personal data if your forms collect it. Attachment file names and contents are not stored, only the number of files.
  • Request details. Request key, summary, project, status, assignee name and Atlassian account ID, request type, and created and updated dates.
  • Configuration. Data sources, question mappings, saved reports with their owner's Atlassian account ID, and settings.
  • Access checks. For each viewer, whether they may view each form, kept for up to two hours.

For this data, your organization is the controller and LimeVibe Limited acts as a processor. Atlassian hosts and processes it on our behalf as the Forge platform provider.

Purpose

To provide FormScope's reporting features to your site. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling or training AI models.

Where data is stored

In Atlassian-hosted Forge storage. Forge keeps app data in the same location as your Atlassian site and encrypts it on disk. FormScope adds no other storage.

Atlassian (Forge platform)Hosting, compute and storage for FormScope

Logs and diagnostics

FormScope writes operational logs to Atlassian's Forge logging, which we can view to troubleshoot. These logs contain identifiers, counts and error codes. Form answers, request summaries, JQL, names and credentials are kept out of them. FormScope uses no analytics or tracking services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). For data FormScope stores in your Jira site, contact your organization's Jira admin first; they can delete it at any time. You can also contact us at contact@limevibelimited.com.
